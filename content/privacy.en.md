This Privacy Policy explains information collected and used by **gnosyslambda's log** (https://gnosyslambda.github.io, the “Blog”).

## Information collected

**Analytics (Google Analytics)**

- The Blog may collect anonymous usage statistics such as visits, page views, and time on page.
- The Blog does not use those statistics to identify individual visitors.
- You can opt out of Google Analytics with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Advertising (Google AdSense)**

- The Blog may display advertising through Google AdSense.
- Google may use cookies to show interest-based advertising.
- You can manage interest-based advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

**Comments**

- When a visitor posts a comment, the Blog collects the display name and comment content.
- Comment passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes; plaintext passwords are not retained.
- Comment data is stored through Supabase.

**Page-view counter**

- The Blog stores a page path to aggregate page-view counts.
- This does not identify an individual visitor.

## Cookies

The Blog may use cookies for Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Disabling cookies in your browser may limit some functionality.

## Retention and deletion

- Comment data is retained while the Blog is operated.
- To request deletion of a comment, use the deletion function in the Blog or contact the author through GitHub.
